We build technologies, conduct research, connect people, and preserve knowledge to advance human survival on Earth and beyond.

What is DH?

DH is an organization working at the intersection of science, medicine, engineering, and exploration to address problems whose solutions don't yet exist. This includes cancer, mental health, AI, ML, robotics, aerospace, and engineering.

Philosophy

Humanity's greatest advances have come from doing what we thought was impossible and what we did not yet understand. Diseases once considered incurable became treatable. Technologies like airplanes proved we could fly. Humans left Earth and began exploring beyond it. DH exists to pursue what comes next. We believe the unknown is not a boundary — it's an infinite invitation for humans to do something.
